Entry for make.fun competition `u-po-pu-one-star-five-stars-for-the-food-f9166ee7`:
one sentence, at most 200 characters — a one-star review of Earth by an
alien tourist that still compliments the food.

`main.py` writes the sentence to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`):

> One star: the locals worship glowing rectangles, queue for burnt water, and argue with strangers they will never meet — but the noodles alone are worth the interstellar parking ticket.

184 characters, one sentence. The alien reads humans from outside —
glowing rectangles, burnt water, strangers argued with but never met —
then the turn: the noodles alone justify the trip, parking ticket and all.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py            # writes /output/entry.txt (or $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt)
```
